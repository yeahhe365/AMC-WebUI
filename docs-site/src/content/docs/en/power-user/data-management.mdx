# Session & Data Management

With our Local-First philosophy, your assets remain completely inside your browser. AMC WebUI provides comprehensive organization tools.

---

## 1. Full-Text Search
- Global search input at the top of the sidebar.
- Indexes both session titles and **complete message body contents** for immediate query matching and auto-scrolling to exact dialogue turns.

---

## 2. Grouping & Pinning
- **Pin Conversations**: Pin critical topics to the top of the sidebar via context menus or `/pin`.
- **Custom Folders**: Organize chats into custom folders (e.g., "Research", "Coding", "Drafts").

---

## 3. Backup, Export & Migration
Under **Settings -> Data Management**:
- **Full JSON Export**: Back up all conversations, custom model configurations, and shortcuts into a portable `.json` snapshot.
- **Export Single Chats**: Export individual chats into **Markdown (.md)**, **PNG screenshots**, or **PDF**.
- **Restore & Import**: Seamlessly restore your entire workstation onto another computer or browser context.
- **Storage Cleanup**: Selectively prune cached temporary files while protecting critical text archives.
